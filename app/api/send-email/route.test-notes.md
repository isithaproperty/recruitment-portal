# Verification checklist

1. Deploy the feature branch with `RESEND_API_KEY` available to Preview and Production.
2. Confirm `isithaproperty.co.za` is verified in the same Resend team as the key.
3. Send a client submission and confirm the email appears in Resend Sending logs.
4. Submit a public application and confirm the internal notification arrives.
5. Save Hold / Do not interview / Interview on a client review link and confirm the internal notification arrives.
6. Submit interview feedback and confirm the outcome notification arrives.
7. Confirm the browser never receives or logs `RESEND_API_KEY`.
